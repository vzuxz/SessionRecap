# Session Recap

When you leave a level you get a quick recap of the session:

- when you played it
- how many attempts you did and how long you actually played
- your best run, and whether it was a new best or a completion
- the percent you died at the most

Hit the clock button on the level page (next to the copy button) to see every session on that level.

Practice mode and start positions don't count for best runs or deaths. Platformer levels show attempts and time.
